step 3 done, feature engineering added to the text vectorization pipeline and built a hybrid feature extraction 
pipeline combining classical NLP with custom legal domain engineering. 
Extracted unigrams and bigrams (ngram_range=(1, 2)) to capture multi-word legal collocations
Engineered 8 dense numerical signals for each clause with Character length (log-scaled), average word length, and uppercase token 
ratio and Modal verb density and Term densities for liability/defect, termination/default, jurisdiction/courts, and 
payment/financial terms.
Combined sparse text features and dense domain metrics via FeatureUnion into a single 15,599-dimensional feature space and saved
the fitted transformer to vectorizer.joblib for identical feature mapping during model training and unseen contract inference.
